# 🚗 Rideshare Ambassadors

Rideshare Ambassadors may create approved vehicle-based awareness through RIAH Pathway branding, seat covers, back-seat digital displays, brochures, information cards, QR codes, referral links, webinars, community events, and verified referrals.

## Eligibility

Participants must complete required Ambassador orientation and marketing training, use only approved vehicle and marketing materials, follow applicable platform, vehicle, property, and local requirements, avoid interfering with passenger safety, avoid unwanted pressure on passengers, use assigned attribution tools, and submit verifiable activity evidence.

## Benefit Formula

**100 approved points = 1% eligible tuition + 1% eligible products.**

Maximum: **2,500 points = 25% tuition + 25% products**.

| Staff approved booth | 50 |
| Complete approved outreach campaign | 25 |
| Qualified referral | 10 |

Installation points are normally awarded once per approved installation rather than repeatedly for continued display.
