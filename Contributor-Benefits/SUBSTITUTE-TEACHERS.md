# 🍎 Substitute Teacher Ambassadors

Substitute Teacher Ambassadors may support approved school-community outreach, education events, career fairs, presentations, webinars, referrals, and marketing.

## Eligibility

Participants must complete required Ambassador orientation and training, use approved RIAH Pathway materials, obtain required school, district, venue, or employer permission, avoid marketing during instructional time without authorization, accurately represent RIAH Pathway, use assigned QR and referral tracking, protect student and prospect information, and submit verifiable activity evidence.

## Benefit Formula

**100 approved points = 1% eligible tuition + 1% eligible products.**

Maximum: **2,500 points = 25% tuition + 25% products**.
