# 📦 Delivery Ambassadors

Delivery Ambassadors may support approved community visibility through vehicle branding, QR codes, referral links, brochures, information cards, community events, booths, webinars, outreach, organizational introductions, and verified referrals.

## Eligibility

Participants must complete required Ambassador orientation and marketing training, use approved materials, follow applicable platform, delivery, property, and local requirements, use assigned attribution tools, and submit verifiable activity evidence. Marketing materials must not be placed inside customer food, packages, mailboxes, private property, or delivery orders unless specifically permitted.

## Benefit Formula

**100 approved points = 1% eligible tuition + 1% eligible products.**

Maximum: **2,500 points = 25% tuition + 25% products**.
