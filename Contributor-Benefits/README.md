## Master Benefit Structure

- Contributors and Ambassadors: 100 approved points = 1%; up to 25% eligible tuition and 25% eligible products.
- Education and Experiential graduates: successful completion = guaranteed 10% eligible tuition; continued approved milestones may increase the tuition benefit up to 50%; no Graduate Product Benefit.
- Eligible Partner Employees: 15% eligible tuition and 15% eligible products under applicable partner terms.
- Applicable Partner or Pillar Product Benefit: up to 25% eligible products where specifically authorized.

All points and benefits require applicable verification and remain subject to RIAH Pathway eligibility, benefit, and discount rules.
