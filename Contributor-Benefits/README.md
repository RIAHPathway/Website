## III. 👑 General Eligibility

1. Register or participate through an applicable approved RIAH Pathway program.
2. Maintain an identifiable participant profile.
3. Use an assigned participant, contributor, ambassador, referral, graduate, or partner identifier when required.
4. Follow applicable program instructions.
5. Use approved RIAH Pathway branding and marketing materials.
6. Accurately represent RIAH Pathway programs, products, services, pathways and opportunities.
7. Never make unauthorized promises regarding admission, accreditation, authorization, financial aid, employment, certification, licensure or educational outcomes.
8. Follow applicable school, employer, venue, platform and property requirements.
9. Obtain required permission before setting up booths, distributing materials, displaying materials, or representing RIAH at organized events.
10. Submit required evidence for point-bearing activities.
11. Use assigned QR codes and referral links when attribution is required.
12. Protect student, prospect, customer, employee and organizational information.
13. Never fabricate referrals, conversions, purchases, attendance, contributions, testing, events or activities.
14. Complete required training before publicly representing RIAH Pathway.
15. Use reasonably current approved materials.
16. Follow applicable RIAH policies.
17. Have qualifying activities verified before permanent points are awarded.

## IV. 🧮 Contributor and Ambassador Points Formula

**⭐ 100 approved points = 🎓 1% eligible tuition + 🛍️ 1% eligible products.**

Only complete 100-point thresholds increase the benefit. Remaining points carry forward. Example: **760 points = 7% tuition + 7% products + 60 carryforward points**.

## VIII. 🎯 GitHub Contribution Scoring Standards

Point awards consider scope, complexity, meaningful effort, completion, accuracy, verification, implementation, documentation, accessibility, responsiveness, integration and risk. Points measure accepted contribution value rather than raw GitHub activity.

## XVIII. 🔗 QR Codes, Referral Links and Attribution

Each applicable Ambassador receives an individually attributable QR code and referral link. Approved destinations may include the RIAH Pathway website, program pages, educational webinars, application pages, event registration, product pages, information-request forms and approved landing pages.

**Ambassador → QR or Link → Engagement → Lead → Webinar → Application → Enrollment or Purchase**

Conversions must be traceable to the assigned identifier or otherwise verified. Self-referrals do not qualify. Duplicate referrals do not create duplicate conversion awards.

## XX–XXI. 🎓🛍️ Contributor and Ambassador Benefits

100 points = 1%; 2,500 points = 25% maximum. Approved points from multiple authorized Contributor or Ambassador tracks may contribute to the same total. Benefits cannot be exchanged for cash or ordinarily transferred and remain subject to applicable eligibility and discount-stacking requirements.

## XXII. 🛡️ Verification and Anti-Abuse

Verification may include GitHub records, pull requests, issues, project records, event registration, event check-in, appropriate event evidence, Ambassador activity records, QR analytics, referral analytics, webinar registration, application attribution, enrollment attribution, product-order attribution, approved campaign records and maintainer or coordinator approval.

Zero-point activity includes fake or duplicate referrals, self-referrals, fake purchases, fraudulent transactions, fabricated events or attendance, QR manipulation, automated fake traffic, spam, plagiarism, duplicate GitHub submissions, unauthorized copyrighted materials, fabricated testing or research, empty commits, cosmetic commit splitting, unauthorized school or vehicle marketing, unauthorized branding, misleading program claims, pressure-based marketing, abandoned work, rejected work, and restricted or confidential information.

One underlying activity normally receives one primary award unless separate point-bearing milestones or deliverables are independently verified.

## XXIII. ⏳ Status Workflow

**⏳ Pending → 👀 Under Review → 🔄 Revision if Required → ✅ Approved → 👑 Credited**

Pending work receives no permanent points. Revision may be completed when permitted. Rejected work receives zero points.

## XXVI–XXXII. 🎓 Education and Experiential Graduates

Successful completion of an eligible Education or Experiential Pathway establishes **1,000 Graduate Points and a guaranteed 10% eligible tuition benefit**. Graduate products receive no discount through this Graduate Benefit. Each additional 100 approved Graduate Points adds 1% eligible tuition, through **5,000 points = 50% maximum**. See [STUDENTS.md](./STUDENTS.md) for the full 10%–50% schedule, activities and eligibility.

## XXXIII–XXXV. 🤝 Partners

Eligible Partner Employees receive **15% eligible tuition and 15% eligible products** under applicable terms. A separate authorized Partner or Pillar Product Benefit may reach **up to 25% eligible products** where the written partnership terms specifically authorize it. See [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md).

## XXXVII. 👑 Master Benefit Logic

**Contributors and Ambassadors:** Approved Contribution → Verified Points → 100 Points = 1% → 1%–25% Milestones → up to 25% Tuition + up to 25% Products.

**Graduates:** Complete Entire Eligible Pathway → Automatic 1,000 Points → Guaranteed 10% Tuition → Continued Approved Graduate Participation → 100 Additional Points = +1% → 5,000 Total Points → 50% Tuition Maximum → No Graduate Product Discount.

**Partners:** Verified Partner Affiliation → Applicable Written Partnership → Eligible Partner Employee → 15% Tuition + 15% Products. Separate authorized Partner or Pillar Product Benefit → up to 25% Products.

## XXXIX. 👑 Benefit Caps

GitHub Contributors, Community Contributors, Community Ambassadors, Substitute Teacher Ambassadors, Rideshare Ambassadors and Delivery Ambassadors: **up to 25% tuition + up to 25% products**.

Education and Experiential Graduates: **guaranteed 10% tuition after successful complete-pathway completion; up to 50% tuition; no Graduate Product Benefit**.

Eligible Partner Employees: **15% tuition + 15% products**. Applicable Partner or Pillar Product Benefit: **up to 25% products where authorized**.
