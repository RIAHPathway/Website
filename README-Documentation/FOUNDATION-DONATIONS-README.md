## LXVII. 🎓 DONOR SCHOLARSHIP, GRANT, OR STIPEND BENEFIT

RIAH intends to connect donations directly with student opportunity.

A qualifying donor may designate the applicable donation to support a Scholarship, Grant, or Stipend.

The donor may choose to name the Scholarship, Grant, or Stipend, have the award associated with the donor's name or applicable organization, or remain anonymous.

RIAH intends to provide an applicable matching contribution corresponding to the donor's donation amount for qualifying Scholarship, Grant, or Stipend support.

Example:

Donor Contribution — $1,000

RIAH Matching Amount — $1,000

Potential Combined Student Support — $2,000

Final award, matching, eligibility, administration, timing, and distribution remain subject to applicable RIAH scholarship, grant, stipend, governance, financial, and program requirements.

## LXVIII. 🏅 ACCREDITATION AND AUTHORIZATION DEVELOPMENT

RIAH is currently in the active pursuit and development stage.

RIAH is not representing itself as currently holding an accreditation, candidate status, provisional approval, authorization, or recognition that has not actually been received.

RIAH's planning includes pursuit or evaluation of applicable accreditation and recognition pathways such as:

| # | Item |
|---:|---|
| 1 | American Bar Association |
| 2 | ABET |
| 3 | Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges |
| 4 | Association for Experiential Education |
| 5 | Distance Education Accrediting Commission |
| 6 | National Security Agency National Centers of Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity |

RIAH's state authorization development begins with Ohio and is intended to expand as applicable to programs, delivery models, institutional structure, and jurisdictions served.

Additional authorization planning may include Ohio postsecondary authorization, High School authorization, GED and HSE requirements, state-specific education requirements, distance-education authorization, NC-SARA participation when applicable and eligible, state-by-state requirements, applicable legal education requirements, and other required approvals.

A finalized public dollar-cost schedule for every targeted accreditor and state authorizer should be published only as applicable application fees, site-visit expenses, annual fees, legal expenses, authorization expenses, bonds, professional services, and other required costs are formally established.
